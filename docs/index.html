What it is

The paper ledger, reimagined.

In a savings group, one person usually keeps the paper ledger — recording who paid, who borrowed, and what is owed. That works fine until the book is lost, the treasurer is absent, or two people remember things differently.

vBank is that same ledger, but it lives on every member's phone at once. There is no company holding your money and no server holding your records. vBank doesn't move money — cash is still handled in the room — but it records what happened, proves who wrote it, and makes sure every member's copy agrees.

vBank is a record-keeping tool, not a bank. It doesn't hold funds or make payments. Your group's money stays in your group's hands.

How it works

Trust the records,
not the people.

01

No sign-up

Open the app, type your name. That's it. No phone number, no email, no password.

02

Signed entries

Every entry is signed by the person who wrote it. Every phone checks the signature. Fakes are rejected.

03

Encrypted

A shared passphrase encrypts everything. Without it, the data is gibberish. Shared in person, never over the link.

04

Direct sync

Phones find each other and exchange records directly. No server in the middle. No outage, no bill.

05

Locked locally

Your phone's data is encrypted with a key in the device's secure chip. Lost phone = no leaked books.

06

Clean conflicts

Two people edit offline? Newest wins, everyone converges. No manual merging.

Keep your passphrase safe. Nobody — not us, not your co-members — can recover it. Without it, a group's records can't be read on a new phone.

FAQ

Questions? We've got answers.

Does vBank hold or move our money?

No. Cash is handled by your group exactly as it is now. vBank records what was collected, lent and repaid. It has no payment feature and no wallet.

Do we need internet?

Not to use it. You only need connectivity when you want phones to exchange records — and even then it can wait. A Wi-Fi hotspot at the meeting is enough.

What happens if I lose my phone?

Restore your backup file with its PIN on the new phone and you're back, including your group keys. Without a backup, your identity can't be recreated — but your group can re-add you as a new member.

Can a member secretly change the books?

No. Members can only read. Anything an admin writes is signed and numbered. A tampered or unsigned record is rejected by every other phone.

Who can see our records?

Only people who have your group's passphrase. What travels between phones is encrypted. Without the passphrase, it's meaningless.

Is there a fee?

No. No subscription, no transaction fee, no advertising, and no analytics.

Deleting your data

vBank stores your records on your own device only — there's no account to close and no copy held by us. To delete everything, open Settings → Log out (removes your identity and keys) or uninstall the app. Encrypted records your group already exchanged remain on your co-members' phones — ask your group's admins to remove you from the roster if you want to stop receiving updates.
